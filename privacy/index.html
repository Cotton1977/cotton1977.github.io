Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Kiet for Android

Kiet asks for no account and no personal details. Your games and progress stay on your device. Data only leaves it through Google's ads (a short ad between games every few games, and the videos you choose to watch), through Google Play and RevenueCat (when you buy “No ads”), and as anonymous usage statistics.

Who is responsible

Kiet is developed by Cotton77. For any question or request about your data, write to cotton77.games@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzles in progress, results, statistics, achievements, settings and whether you own “No ads” are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If you have Android backup turned on, they are included in your own device backup (managed by Google under your account), so you can recover them on a new phone. The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device and sends nothing.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Kiet shows no banners. A short ad appears between games every 3 games, never during a game, and a short video plays when you choose it, to see a hint or unlock an older daily puzzle. “No ads” removes them all. To serve and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message appears before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your card details. To confirm and restore your purchase, RevenueCat receives your purchase history, an anonymous app user ID, and your language and currency. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Anonymous usage statistics (Aptabase)

To learn which puzzles are played and where the app can improve, Kiet sends a few anonymous events to Aptabase, on servers in Germany: app opened, tutorial finished, puzzle started and solved (type, level and number of hints), hint unlocked, video watched and purchase made. Each event also carries the app version, the operating system, the device model, the language, and the country and region, which Aptabase estimates from the IP address without storing it. No device identifier, advertising ID or account is collected, and the events cannot be linked to you. See the Aptabase privacy policy.

Children

Kiet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to the address above. To delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, tell us and we will delete them. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with a new effective date.

No es recull cap identificador del dispositiu, ni l'identificador de publicitat, ni cap compte, i els esdeveniments no es poden relacionar amb tu. Consulta la política de privacitat d'Aptabase.

Canvis
